# Report templates

Write three views from the same facts. Keep facts, assumptions and unknowns separate.

## Operations report (plant manager, shift lead)

- What happened to the process, in plain words
- Current process state and safety status
- Actions operations must take or approve now
- What to watch on the HMI and in the field
- When the next update will come

## SOC report (security analysts)

- Timeline with timestamps and sources
- Affected hosts, accounts and zones
- Indicators and data sources
- ATT&CK for ICS mapping with confidence
- Hypothesis ranking and the next checks
- Evidence preserved and chain of custody status

## Executive report (management, board)

- One-paragraph summary
- Severity and business impact (service, safety, environment, cost if known)
- Regulatory position (NIS2 deadlines, other duties)
- Decisions needed from management
- Next update time

Keep the executive report under 200 words.
