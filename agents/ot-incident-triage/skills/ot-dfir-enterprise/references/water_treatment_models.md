# Water and waste water process notes

## Typical process stages

Raw water intake and storage, coagulation and flocculation, sedimentation, filtration, disinfection (chlorine, UV), treated water storage and distribution, plus sludge handling. Waste water adds screening, biological treatment and discharge monitoring.

## Safety-relevant variables

- Tank and reservoir levels (overflow, pump dry running)
- Chemical dosing rates (for example chlorine, sodium hydroxide, coagulants): overdose or underdose affects public health
- Residual chlorine, pH and turbidity at the outlet
- Pump status and pressure in distribution
- Discharge quality limits for waste water

## Useful cross-checks

- Level: compare the level transmitter with a second sensor, a local sight glass or the inflow and outflow balance from flow meters.
- Dosing: compare the dosing setpoint, pump speed and the measured residual downstream.
- Operator view: compare HMI values with historian raw values and field readings.

## Plausible non-malicious causes

Fouled or drifting sensors, failed transmitters, air in lines, stuck valves, power dips, communication loss between RTU and SCADA, recent maintenance or recalibration.
