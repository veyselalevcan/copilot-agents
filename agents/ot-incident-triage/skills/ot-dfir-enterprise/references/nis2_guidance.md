# NIS2 reporting check

Applies to essential and important entities in the EU under Directive (EU) 2022/2555. Sectors include energy, transport, drinking water, waste water, health, manufacturing of critical products and others. National transposition rules may add detail, so the final call belongs to legal and the national CSIRT or competent authority.

## Significant incident test (Article 23)

An incident is significant if it has caused or can cause severe operational disruption of the service or financial loss for the entity, or has affected or can affect others by causing considerable material or non-material damage.

## Reporting clock (Article 23)

| Report | Deadline |
|---|---|
| Early warning | Within 24 hours of becoming aware; say whether the incident is suspected to be unlawful or malicious, or could have cross-border impact |
| Incident notification | Within 72 hours of becoming aware; update the early warning, give an initial assessment, severity, impact and indicators of compromise where available |
| Intermediate report | On request of the CSIRT or authority |
| Final report | Within one month of the incident notification; detailed description, root cause or threat type, mitigations, cross-border impact. A progress report applies if the incident is still ongoing |

## What to output

- Whether the entity looks in scope (ask if unknown)
- Whether the incident looks significant, with the reason
- The time the entity became aware, and the resulting deadlines
- Information to collect now for the reports
- A reminder to confirm with legal and the CSIRT
